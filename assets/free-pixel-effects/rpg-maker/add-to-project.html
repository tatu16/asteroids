Free Pixel Effects for RPG Maker MV and MZ

Adds all animations to your project in one go: the sheets go in img/animations, and the animations go into the database after the ones you already have. Nothing else in your project changes.

1. Close RPG Maker

Save your project, then close the editor. If it's open, it writes over the database the next time it saves, and the new animations disappear.

2. Choose your project

Pick the folder that has Game.rmmzproject (MZ) or Game.rpgproject (MV) in it.

Sound (optional)

Plays on the first frame. These are your project's own sound effects.

2. Your browser can't open a folder

Chrome and Edge can add everything for you. In this browser:

  1. Copy the PNG files from this pack's img/animations folder into your project's img/animations folder.
  2. Choose your project's data/Animations.json here. You'll get a new one back.
  3. Put the new file in your project's data folder in place of the old one. Keep the old one somewhere as a backup.

Good to know

  • MZ needs version 1.4.0 or later (that's when MZ started playing these sheet animations). MV works as is.
  • Each sheet is the effect scaled up 3 times with hard pixels, so one pixel of art is 3 screen pixels. That matches 16 px art on RPG Maker's 48 px tiles.
  • They play at RPG Maker's sheet animation speed (15 frames a second) and run as long as the original effect.
  • Add it again later and it updates the same animations instead of adding copies.
  • Before it changes anything it saves your old database as Animations.json.backup- plus the date, next to your project file. Right after adding, Undo this puts everything back. Later on: close RPG Maker, rename the backup to Animations.json and put it in the data folder in place of the new one.

What you'll get

Each one plays here the way RPG Maker plays it. Click one to play it again.
